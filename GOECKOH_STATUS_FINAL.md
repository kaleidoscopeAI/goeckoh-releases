## 🔬 THE EXACT SYSTEM (From Your PDF)

### Core Innovation Implemented

**User speaks with error** (e.g., "I wan go park")
    ↓
**Behavior Monitor** detects: ANXIOUS state, Arousal=0.8, Valence=-0.4
    ↓
**Crystalline Heart** calculates: Coherence=0.4, GCL=0.35
    ↓
**Feature Gate**: SIMPLE_CORRECTIONS (GCL < 0.6)
    ↓
**Inner Voice Engine** generates:
  1. [CALMING] "I am safe. I can breathe."
  2. [CORRECTION] "I can say: I want to go to the park"
    ↓
**Voice Crystal** synthesizes in user's EXACT voice
    ↓
**Cognitive Gating**: Play calming first, then correction
    ↓
**User hears** correction in own voice → Inner dialogue triggered
    ↓
**Voice Harvesting**: Update model with good samples
    ↓
**AMFL Tracking**: Monitor progress over time
